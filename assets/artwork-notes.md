# Island artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These local assets are used
directly by the website, with no runtime image-generation dependency.

## Shared scene brief

A single standalone landscape 16:10 production illustration. The original
two-island image was used as a style reference. Premium children's picture-book
gouache texture, hand-painted contours, whimsical depth, and detailed perimeter.
A broad island in calm water, seen overhead at a gentle three-quarter angle,
filling most of the frame. Keep the central meadow open for six HTML lesson
markers and an SVG path. No rendered paths, text, labels, UI, or characters.
Match the warm-neutral teacher interface, rounded Nunito type, and pink/blue
categories.

## locomotor-island.png

Rose pink #E98ABB and pale pink #FCE7F2 identity. Pink-blossom trees, berry-pink
hills, lavender peaks, a small waterfall, open mint-green meadow, cream beaches,
and pastel turquoise water. Pastel springtime storybook adventure.

## object-control-island.png

Ocean blue #2F80ED and pale blue #DCEBFF identity. Jewel-blue bay, blue hills,
teal foliage, palms, a small lagoon and waterfall, a cream lighthouse, and a
small ball and basket at the coast. Open aquamarine meadow, cream sand rim, and
painted white wavelets.

## explorer-fox.png

The original explorer fox was used as a character reference. A standalone,
transparent-background picture-book fox sprite, readable at about 64px.
Large smiling orange-and-cream head, tiny body, rounded ears, ocean-blue
backpack, sage explorer hat, and three-quarter pose facing right. Full body,
feet, and tail in frame. Hand-painted matte gouache texture, crisp silhouette,
without ground, scenery, text, or loose objects.
